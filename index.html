You are standing on one small patch of Manhattan.
This planet will see twelve billion years. You won't.

Walk around with WASD and the mouse. Drag the line at the bottom of the screen (or scroll, or hold Q / E) to move through time — from the molten birth of the Earth to the day the Sun swallows it. Each age is shown where it left its mark: the name of the place is in the top corner.

On a phone: left thumb walks, right thumb looks. Sound on is recommended. Notice how little of the line is ours.
